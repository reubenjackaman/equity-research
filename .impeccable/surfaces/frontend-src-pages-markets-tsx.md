# Value Finch site redesign

Scope: the whole web front end (home/markets, company screens, learn, puzzle, info). Visitor mode: Persuade on the home page, Operate on company and market screens. Audience: beginner investors, who find the current terminal intimidating; the site must also impress.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every company is a property deed card a beginner already knows how to read: a coloured sector band, a name, and a ruled table of what it is worth. Refuses the dark trading-terminal dashboard and the white fintech card grid.

OWN-WORLD: The board's pale green field as page ground; white card stock with a thin inset frame line and 6px corners; flat saturated sector bands (one colour per sector) as the only colour on a card; black ink, ruled value tables with tabular numerals; heavy condensed caps for card titles, a plain grotesk for reading. Primary action is solid ink that reverses hard to the band colour. Raises: colour only in the band, reading fields black on white (from Cloud Edge); one identical card anatomy everywhere so rows compare at a glance (from VU Meter Bridge); the DCF verdict box stays closed until inputs are complete (from Teen Magazine Quiz); active states are hard two-state reversals, never fades (from Cabaret Bill).

STORY: A beginner sees that a company can be read like a card, searches one, and finds plain-English explanations beside every number; they leave feeling capable.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Green field. Left: the finch mark, one plain headline, one sentence, and the working company search as the dominant control with example companies. Right: a fanned hand of three live deed cards (today's movers) at real card scale, each with sector band, price, day change and key numbers, flippable.
